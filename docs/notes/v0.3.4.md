A compatibility patch: the upload hints and the desktop window are readable in
browsers that do not know `color-mix()`.

## Fixed

- The **About uploading** box (and the info icon next to its heading) came out
  with no border, no tint and no icon in browsers older than Safari 16.2,
  Chrome 111 or Firefox 113 — including the webview of macOS 12–15. It is now
  plain `rgba()` over the accent colour.
- The desktop window's **error strip** and the little tiles in **Recent
  uploads** had the same problem on those webviews; both keep their colours now.
- The upload **progress bar** no longer loses its colour, and the drag frame
  keeps its tint, when `color-mix()` is missing.
- The share page no longer depends on `inset:`, the unprefixed `mask` or
  `replaceChildren()`, so older phones can still refresh the list.

## Notes

- **macOS 12 or later** is now required. Catalina and Big Sur webviews lack flex
  `gap` and `inset`; there the window would look broken rather than plainer.
- CI now runs `scripts/check-css-fallbacks.mjs`: a `color-mix()` without a plain
  declaration in front of it fails the build.
